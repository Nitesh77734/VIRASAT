OUR WORK

Keeping Jaipur's Heritage Alive

From historic buildings and traditional crafts to cultural traditions and community spaces, Virasat works with local people to protect the heritage that makes Jaipur unique.

Join Our Work →

WHAT WE DO

Heritage is more than buildings.

It lives in the hands of artisans, in neighbourhood streets, in traditional celebrations, and in the stories passed from one generation to another.

Virasat brings communities, volunteers, artisans and young people together to protect these living traditions and make Jaipur's heritage meaningful for future generations.

OUR APPROACH

Change begins with the community.

We believe heritage conservation works best when local communities are part of the process.

We listen, document, collaborate and create opportunities for people to take an active role in protecting the places and traditions they call home.

01

Understand local stories, needs and experiences.

02
